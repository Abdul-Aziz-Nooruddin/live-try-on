Monthly Software Plan

Your $25/mo or $35/mo seat unlocks full access to the software: live AI teleprompter, SpeedDialer engine, CRM, unlimited call recording/transcription storage, and 1 free dedicated phone number.

02

Usage Credits (100 = $1 USD)

100 credits equal $1.00 USD. Every new account receives $5 USD worth of credits for free in your first month (500 credits) to test and dial immediately. Outbound carrier minutes and real-time AI transcription are deducted from credits at transparent wholesale rates (~2 to 3 credits/min).
